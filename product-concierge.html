The Story

From the ERATO Design series (ED-11): a film turned into its colour chart. Forty-two squares of plum, vermilion and mustard pulled from the frame, with the film still set in the middle like a window. Printed on the back of a washed charcoal tee.

The Print

DTG-printed on premium 280gsm cotton, oversized fit, then stone-washed so the fabric looks lived-in from the first wear. The print sits on the back; the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart.
