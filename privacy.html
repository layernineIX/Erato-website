Privacy

What we collect

When you place an order, PayPal collects your name, email, shipping address and payment details and passes the order information to us so we can send your order. We do not see or store your card number.

What we use it for

  • To prepare, ship and support your order.
  • To contact you about that order.

Payments

Payments are handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy. You can pay by debit or credit card without a PayPal account.

Your bag

Your bag is stored in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. We do not send it anywhere until you pay.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with PayPal and with the delivery companies that carry your order.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, message us on Instagram: @eratodesign.store.
