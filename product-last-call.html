The Story

From the ERATO Design series (ED-01): a famous 1942 painting of a diner at night, turned into its colour chart. Forty-two squares of deep green, olive and rust, with the painting set in the middle. Printed on the back of a bone tee, so the dark of the scene comes through.

The Print

DTG-printed on premium 280gsm cotton, oversized fit, then stone-washed so the fabric looks lived-in from the first wear. The print sits on the back; the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart.
