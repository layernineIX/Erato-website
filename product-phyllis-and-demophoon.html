The Story

Burne-Jones, 1870: Phyllis, turned into an almond tree, holds on to Demophoön as blossom falls around them. We set the painting large on a black tee and cut it with one sharp diagonal, so the old romance reads as something modern.

The Print

DTG-printed at A3 size on premium cotton, oversized fit, so the colours of the painting sit clean against the black.
