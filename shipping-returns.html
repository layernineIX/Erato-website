Shipping and returns

Shipping

Our pieces ship from Southeast Asia. Shipping is charged once per order, based on where we send it. You choose your destination and Standard or Express delivery in the bag before paying. Express uses a faster courier.

The shipping address you give at payment must be in the destination you chose. We email you a tracking number when your order ships. Delivery times vary by destination.

Import duties and taxes

Orders sent outside Malaysia may be charged import duties or taxes by your country. These are not included in our prices and are paid by the buyer.

Returns

  • You can return t-shirts, tote bags and other unworn items within 14 days of delivery.
  • The item must be unworn, unwashed and in its original condition.
  • Message us on Instagram first so we can give you the return details. You pay the cost of sending it back, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong one.
  • Once we receive and check the return, we refund the item price to your original payment method.

Prints and handmade items

Prints and handmade items, such as the Loom shoes, cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty or damaged. If that happens, send us photos within 7 days of delivery and we will replace the item or refund you.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, message us with photos and we will put it right at our cost.

Your legal rights

None of this affects your statutory rights under the consumer law where you live.
