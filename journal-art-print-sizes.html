Which art print size is right for your wall?

Choosing a print size is mostly about the wall and how far away you will stand from it. Here are the four sizes we offer.

The sizes

  • A4 is 21 x 29.7 cm (8.3 x 11.7 in). USD 29. A good size for a desk, a shelf, a small gallery wall or a gift.
  • A3 is 29.7 x 42 cm (11.7 x 16.5 in). USD 39. The easiest size to place: it suits a hallway, a bedroom wall or above a desk.
  • A2 is 42 x 59.4 cm (16.5 x 23.4 in). USD 52. A real statement over a sofa, a bed or a console table.
  • A1 is 59.4 x 84.1 cm (23.4 x 33.1 in). USD 68. For a large wall, where the print becomes the main thing in the room.

Quick rules

  • Small space or first print: A3.
  • Gallery wall with several pictures: a mix of A4 and A3.
  • One print over a sofa: A2 or A1. The picture should be about two thirds of the width of the furniture below it.
  • Hanging height: the centre of the picture at about eye level, roughly 145 cm from the floor.

Framing

A simple natural wood frame with a white mount lets the image speak. It is the look we use for Riviera, a 1950s pin-up in a striped swimsuit against pure red. The mount gives the picture room to breathe, and the wood warms up the red.

What we have

Our prints include Riviera, Meridian, a bold geometric piece in blue, ochre and green, and Don't Look, a black and white portrait. Each comes in the four sizes above. See the prints.
