ERATO — Art Direction v1

Concept: Playful surreal editorial

1. The World

ERATO stages art history as fashion — museum halls, painted portraiture, and cinema stills reimagined as a clothing and accessories studio. Not a streetwear label: a design studio treating the archive as raw material.

3. Typography

Jost — minimal, geometric, chic. Close to the logo's character without imitating it directly. Light weights (300/400) for body and headlines, uppercase tracked-out labels for eyebrows/tags/nav.

4. Four Core Shot Types

Shot A — Hero: classical statue in colonnade, warm daylight.
Shot B — Cinema Cut: wide film-still composition, moody chiaroscuro.
Shot C — Object: clean studio product shot, gallery treatment.
Shot D — Reinterpretation: painterly Renaissance-style portrait.

5. Layout & Motion

Full-bleed imagery, generous negative space, scroll-triggered reveals, subtle hover scale on collection cards. Nav is transparent over the hero and solidifies on scroll.

6. Voice

Confident, spare, art-historical without being academic. Short declarative sentences. No hype language, no exclamation points.

7. Summary

ERATO = the archive, staged as fashion. Every asset should read as a still from a film that doesn't exist, or a portrait that belongs in a museum but is wearable.
