The Story

Loom is woven the slow way: strips of natural leather knotted by hand, one crossing at a time. No two pairs are exactly alike, and the leather softens and darkens the more you wear it.

Care

Wipe with a dry cloth, keep away from long soaks, and condition the leather now and then. Natural marks in the hide are part of the piece.
