The Story

A court scene from a Mughal miniature, built to be held in one hand and studied close. We printed it at full scale on the back and laid a brushed white calligraphy stroke across it, as if the painting were being signed.

The Print

Screenprinted on heavyweight 240gsm cotton jersey, oversized fit, then stone-washed so the fabric looks lived-in from the first wear. The print sits on the back; the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart.
