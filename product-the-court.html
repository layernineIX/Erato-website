The Court

Premium cotton, DTG print · Natural

USD 44

The Story

The image comes from a Mughal-era court scene — a miniature painting, originally the size of a postcard, built for a hand to hold and eyes to study up close. We pulled it onto the back panel at full scale and let it stay exactly as busy and gold-lit as it always was. The calligraphy overlay reads ERATO in a script built to match the painting's own hand, not sit on top of it like a logo.

The Print

DTG-printed on premium 280gsm cotton, oversized fit. Colors are matched panel-by-panel to the original palette rather than a flat four-color process, which is why the print holds detail at close range.
