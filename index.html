    ERATO is a brand built around one idea: the images worth keeping shouldn't be stuck on a wall. Every piece starts as a reference — a painting, a film still, a forgotten poster — and ends as a T-shirt, a print, an object you keep.

    No trends. No mascots. Just the image, worn.
