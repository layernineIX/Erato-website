About ERATO

ERATO is a clothing and accessories studio built around one idea: art history is not something you hang on a wall — it's something you can wear. Every piece starts as a reference — a painting, a film still, a sculpture — and ends as a T-shirt, a print, an object you keep.

No trends. No mascots. Just the archive, staged as fashion.
