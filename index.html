Not clothes with art printed on them. Art history, staged as fashion.

ERATO treats garments, prints and objects as a medium for visual culture — not surfaces for decoration. Every piece begins with a reference, recontextualized rather than reproduced.

A design studio working in art history, Pop Art, cinema and photography. Not a streetwear label.
