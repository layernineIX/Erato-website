The Story

From the ERATO Design series (ED-09): an 1831 woodblock wave turned into its colour chart. Indigo, slate and the cream of old paper, with the wave itself set in the middle. Printed on the back of a washed charcoal tee, so the sea sits on a dark sky.

The Print

DTG-printed on premium 280gsm cotton, oversized fit, then stone-washed so the fabric looks lived-in from the first wear. The print sits on the back; the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart.
