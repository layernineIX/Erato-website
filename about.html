About

ERATO is a brand. Not a museum, not a history lesson — just images that deserved a body to live on.

We didn't set out to build an archive. We set out to build things worth wearing — and it turned out the most interesting source material was already sitting in paintings, film stills, pulp magazine covers and posters nobody was looking at anymore.

So we took them. Screenprinted them. Put them on heavyweight cotton and canvas, gave them names instead of catalogue numbers, and let them go be worn instead of stored.

That's the whole idea. No trends, no mascots, no seasonal drops chasing a feed. Just images good enough to wear twice.
