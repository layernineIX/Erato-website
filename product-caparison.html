The Story

A caparison is the ornamental cloth a horse wears. Here it is a whole carpet, draped over the animal and left hanging, so the horse looks like a piece of furniture that learned to stand. One quiet, strange image, printed large across the back of a bone tee.

The Print

DTG-printed on premium 280gsm cotton, oversized fit, then stone-washed so the fabric looks lived-in from the first wear. The print sits on the back; the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart.
