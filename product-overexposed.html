Overexposed

Cotton jersey, screenprint · Natural

RM 169

The Story

A black-and-white photograph pushed past the point of subtlety — grain up, contrast up, until a portrait starts reading like a graphic. The ERATO wordmark sits above it in a clean sans, deliberately plain next to all that noise.

The Print

Screenprinted on heavyweight 240gsm cotton jersey, oversized fit. Single-tone halftone print for maximum grain retention.
