Fracture

Cotton jersey, screenprint · Natural

RM 169

The Story

A Cubist study, broken into color blocks of blue, gold and black, with a calligraphy mark running through the fracture lines instead of sitting cleanly on top of them. We shot this one in macro because the print rewards being looked at that close — the weave of the cotton is part of the image, not just a surface it sits on.

The Print

Screenprinted on heavyweight 240gsm cotton jersey, oversized fit. Full graphic runs edge to edge across the chest.
