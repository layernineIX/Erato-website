The Story

A court scene from a Mughal miniature: scholars, musicians and a ruler under a canopy, painted to be held in one hand and studied close. We printed it at full scale on the back and laid the ERATO name across it in white Farsi calligraphy, as if the painting were being signed.

The Print

DTG-printed on premium 280gsm cotton, oversized fit, then stone-washed so the fabric looks lived-in from the first wear. The print sits on the back; the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart.
