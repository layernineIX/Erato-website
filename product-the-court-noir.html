The Court — Noir

Cotton jersey, screenprint · Washed black

RM 169

The Story

Same court scene, same calligraphy as The Court — cast into a stonewashed black instead of natural cotton. The colors of the miniature come forward harder against the dark, like the painting's lit by a single spotlight instead of daylight. For anyone who wanted The Court without wearing cream.

The Print

Screenprinted on heavyweight 240gsm garment-washed cotton jersey, oversized fit. The wash gives every shirt a slightly different fade, so no two are quite identical.
