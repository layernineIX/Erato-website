Journal · Wearable art

What is wearable art?

Wearable art is clothing that carries a real artwork, printed or made with the same care you would give a picture on a wall. The point is not decoration. It is that the thing you put on in the morning is also something worth looking at.

It is older than the t-shirt

Artists and designers have been crossing over for a long time. In 1937 the designer Elsa Schiaparelli worked with Salvador Dalí on a white evening dress with a lobster painted across the skirt. In 1965 Yves Saint Laurent released day dresses built from the grids of Piet Mondrian's paintings. In both cases the garment was a way of putting a picture into the world on a moving body.

Why the t-shirt is now the canvas

The t-shirt is the most worn garment there is, and a back panel gives you something a chest print does not: space. A large print can hold a whole composition, a painting with its borders, a film still, a photograph. Oversized cuts add even more room, and a heavier cotton keeps the image flat and sharp.

What separates art from a graphic

  • Source. The image comes from a painting, a film or a photograph with its own history, not from a stock pack of shapes.
  • Scale. It is printed large enough to read as a picture, not shrunk into a logo.
  • Restraint. The garment stays quiet. One image, one placement, a small mark on the front.
  • Craft. The fabric and the print are good enough that the image is not let down by what it is printed on.

How we do it at ERATO

Every piece starts from an image we cannot stop looking at: a horse draped in a carpet, a Mughal court miniature, a diner at night. We print it large on the back of a 280gsm stone-washed cotton tee, and the front carries only a small ERATO mark over the heart. The collection is seven pieces, each with a name and a story.

How to wear it

Let one piece be the loud thing. Pair an art tee with plain trousers, a good jacket and simple shoes, and the image does the talking. If you are not sure where to start, begin with a colour you already wear: a Bone tee like Caparison sits easily with almost everything, and a Washed Charcoal tee like Illuminated works as a dark base for a bright print.
